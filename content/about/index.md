## Hi, I'm Muhammad Nurfaisal 👋

I'm an Infrastructure & System Engineer based in Bogor, Indonesia, with four years of experience in enterprise infrastructure operations, Linux administration, and monitoring.

For three years, I supported 112+ HPE enterprise servers in a production banking environment for PT Bank Rakyat Indonesia (BRI), as part of the HPE Cloudera project at PT Bringin Inti Teknologi (BIT). My experience covers the infrastructure lifecycle, from server staging, rack mounting, cabling, and OS installation to daily operations, hardware troubleshooting, preventive maintenance, and infrastructure monitoring.

I enjoy working close to the infrastructure itself: investigating hardware alerts, analyzing system logs, troubleshooting Linux connectivity, and helping prevent issues before they affect services.

Outside my professional experience, I'm pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Information Systems at Universitas Terbuka while continuing to develop my skills in automation, containers, and DevOps practices.
